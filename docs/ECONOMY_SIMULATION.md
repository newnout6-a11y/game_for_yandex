# Synthetic economy sensitivity test

Not live player analytics. 1,000 seeded players per skill/ad cell. All outcomes depend on the declared run distributions and the balanced shopping policy. Optional ads add 30 seconds and are watched after half of eligible runs; they grant credits only. Milestones, repeatable missions and all free season credit rewards are included. No clean-ground/airborne-contract bonus or cosmetic duplicate compensation is included.
