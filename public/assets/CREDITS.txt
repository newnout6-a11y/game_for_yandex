Roadster reference-inspired concept (roadster.glb)
Original project artwork, authored in Blender for this project in October 2026.
Editable source: art/roadster.blend; reproducible source: scripts/create_roadster.py.
Based on the user's supplied concept references, not an imported Tesla mesh.
Includes an original angular silver body, detailed wheels, dark canopy,
ivory bucket-seat cabin, yoke, active-wing styling and thrust nozzles.
No Tesla trademarks or badges are included. No manufacturer endorsement implied.
The short hover mechanic is an arcade interpretation, not a real-world specification.
Scenic terrain/landmarks are original project code. The current traffic fleet is original Blender project artwork (see fleet section below).
Legacy Ferrari asset remains in this source bundle with its attribution above;
it is not the active player or traffic model in the current game.

Daylight / underbody revision
- Gameplay illumination now derives its static PMREM from the project's original cloud-sky panorama, rather than the retained Poly Haven HDR. The retained HDR attribution above remains valid.
- Detailed underbody shielding, rails, suspension links, routing and nozzle collars are original project artwork, a visual EV-inspired interpretation rather than manufacturer CAD.
- cold-gas-smoke-atlas.png: eight original Blender Cycles volume renders, generated by scripts/create_smoke_atlas.py and packed by scripts/pack_smoke_atlas.py. Editable source: art/smoke/original-cold-gas-volume.blend. Original-project-artwork; no third-party smoke photographs, renders or sprites copied.

Blender traffic fleet, October 2026
Eight original vehicles: sedan, hatchback, SUV, delivery van, rigid box truck, articulated semi, tow truck and fire apparatus.
Original-project-artwork; no copied third-party vehicle mesh, photo texture, trademark or manufacturer CAD.
Editable sources: art/traffic/{sedan,hatch,suv,van,boxtruck,semi,towtruck,firetruck}.blend.
Reproducible Blender authoring/export: scripts/create_traffic_passenger.py, scripts/create_traffic_heavy.py, scripts/create_traffic_special.py.
The original JavaScript surface builders are authoring inputs only; the game loads the final GLBs in assets/traffic/.
See manifest.json for final binary hashes and docs/FLEET_UPDATE.md for taxonomy and gameplay envelopes.

Landscape stone detail
Original 256x256 locally generated grayscale stone tile shared by distant ridge and near-rock materials.
Trilinear mipmaps, no downloaded rock bitmap, no extra scene pass.

Wheel refinement
Original Blender-authored rounded tire carcasses, recessed alloy/steel rims, brake hardware, hub fasteners, commercial ventilation openings and dual-tire separation. Editable sources and generators are the same eight traffic projects listed above. No third-party wheel models or textures used.

Traffic cabins and mirrors
Original Blender-authored seats, dashboards, steering wheels, inner panels/headliners, cut-through window apertures, mirror panes and separate turn-signal lenses for all eight traffic vehicles. The mirrors use the project's shared sky environment, not per-vehicle live reflection cameras. No external interior or mirror texture/model was used.

Original Blender traffic drivers and interior enclosure refinement (2026-10-17)
All eight traffic vehicles include original seated adult character geometry, sealed
floors/footwells/firewalls/inner wheel housings, steering/grip/stalk markers and
articulated shoulder/elbow/hand groups. Created for this project in the editable
art/traffic/*.blend sources by scripts/create_traffic_{passenger,heavy,special}.py.
No third-party human mesh, character texture, manufacturer CAD or motion capture
was used. Lightweight stylized characters, not photoreal human representations.
